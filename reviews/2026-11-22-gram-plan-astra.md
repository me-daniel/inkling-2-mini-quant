## Reply (verbatim)

1. **A — my leading explanation is that diagonal weight error ranks the wrong quantity.** Within that explanation, I’d rank **(i)** error after the gate and up branches interact, **(ii)** input correlations, and **(iii)** amplification across loop visits and subsequent routing. A gate error matters more when its paired up activation is large. Errors in the two branches can also cancel in their product. Even a full input Gram misses both effects. **Cheapest check:** On identical routed inputs, compare UD and yours against BF16 at three points: the two preactivations, their gated product, and the router-weighted expert mixture after a common down projection. Keep visit labels. Where the ranking first reverses tells you which mechanism deserves work.

2. **A different search objective is plausible. A different source checkpoint ranks lower.** A non-RTN search could preserve activation signs or reduce coherent channel bias while increasing L2. The observed ranking does not identify Unsloth’s algorithm. Check near-zero gate sign flips weighted by their actual product error, and per-channel mean output error. For a different target, compare both encodings to an independently identified candidate checkpoint on matched rows. Your NVFP4 audit establishes your decode exactly, but leaves UD’s gate_up target open. Also rescore a small sample against BF16 logits to test dependence on the Q8 reference. Calibration source alone ranks low given the measured gains. The 8238 counter needs its own visit-accounting audit before interpreting it as a dose.

3. **Check `.scale` first despite its lower explanatory rank.** Quantizer homogeneity says little about whether the evaluator applies the correct scale once, to the correct expert and rows. Probe several experts with widely separated scales using basis vectors spanning IQ block boundaries and one dense input. Compare offline effective-weight products with actual Metal preactivations for *both* files before the nonlinearity. Inspect gate/up order, expert stride, and output ratios. This also catches a transpose hidden by the square fused matrix. A scalar-normalization encoder project is a dead end unless this audit or a rounding ablation finds a material discrepancy.

4. **The Gram test discriminates one narrow hypothesis.** On identical samples, a full/diagonal reversal establishes that off-diagonal input terms reverse the *linear reconstruction* ranking there. It establishes neither the cause of the KL gain nor the value of LDLQ. Score directly with \(\sum\|\Delta W x\|^2\), using uncentered inputs. This needs no Gram allocation or inversion. If storing the upper triangle, double off-diagonal contributions exactly once and verify against direct replay. At 4096 tokens/chunk, uniform routing gives 25,600 assignments/expert outside the loop and 76,800 inside. Inspect actual counts and effective rank for rare experts. Bootstrap chunks, preserve visit labels, and check a Q8-forward subset for dependence on your model’s routes and activations. **My single A experiment** is the staged replay in point 1 on layers 6 and 20, adding the diagonal score on those same samples. Defer encoder work until a pilot beats your encoder downstream using the actual IQ grids and shared scales. QuIP# uses its own codebook and incoherence processing. [QuIP# paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12395268/)

5. **B — first establish that the 25% gain survives deployment.** Only layer 12 overlaps the two reported layer sets. The local comparison also uses a diagonal-rounding control. Verify that control matches the KL baseline. **Cheapest checks, in order:** (i) Decode emitted Q2_0 bytes and compare with the optimizer’s final representable weights, including stored scales. (ii) Replay a labeled expert/token pair through Metal to catch expert permutations, strides, and scale handling. (iii) Verify the down Gram has 1152 input coordinates and its quadratic score matches direct output SSE. (iv) Capture the actual down input after B’s quantized gate activation times up, with routing weights applied at the runtime’s location. Replay each visit. The 11.6 GB chat forward can supply a different input distribution. (v) Compare damping 0.25 with a smaller value on those same held-out inputs. Even a genuine SSE gain can worsen KL through downstream-sensitive error directions or coherent errors across visits.

6. **My single B experiment** is a layer-12-only comparison of the deployed baseline, diagonal control, and packed GPTQ down on the same B base. Measure actual down-output error on identical B inputs and paired end-to-end KL. A local win plus a KL loss indicts the objective at that layer. A KL win redirects attention to layers 13–17 and their interactions.

7. **B’s donor allocation is sensible release engineering and a valid allocation study.** The aggregate swaps establish neither universal donor superiority nor additive tensor gains. Start from the 0.07127 all-Metal file. Screen one byte-matched donor swap on development chunks, preserve associated scales, verify Metal dispatch, then score the assembled file on fresh chunks. Reusing S for selection weakens its bootstrap interval as release evidence. Publish donor revisions, hashes, attribution, applicable redistribution terms, bytes, and measured Metal throughput. Credit the encoding to the donors and the allocation result to your experiment.
