# A. Hypotheses

1. **H1 — Keep the pass-count experiment. Drop the proposed interpretation of the curve.** **V** In varloop, passes 1–5 appeared in late midtraining in a 1:2:6:2:1 ratio. RL used three passes. Every pass starts from the same prelude output, and later passes read gated messages from the previous pass. **Est.** A same-\(P\) reference identifies the fidelity cost of requantizing specified weights at that deployment depth. It cannot identify “counts once,” “once per visit,” or “compounds” from curvature alone. The reference distribution, routing, message gains and distance to the readout all change with \(P\). Even linearly adding coherent logit errors can yield quadratic KL locally. B1 is especially ambiguous because its perturbed output is supplied anew to every pass. A B1 curve cannot establish contraction or a shifted fixed point. [Varloop model card, 20 Nov 2026](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/tml-d24-varloop-research/) · [Architecture and training, 20 Nov 2026](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/) · [Tilted Bowl, 30 Sep 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39277)

2. **H1 — Make the falsifier a difference of differences.** **Est.** On identical token prefixes, define \(D_b(P)=\mathrm{KL}(R_P\Vert Q_{b,P})\), with \(R_P\) the otherwise identical same-engine control. Compare \(D_b(5)-D_b(1)\) between equal-sized looped and unlooped NVFP4 layer groups at one quantization level. Match projection, local/full-attention position and bytes changed where possible. B2/B3 have six layers, B4 two, and B5 four with a different source precision. A precise zero or reversed contrast falsifies stronger loop-core growth for those groups. A wide interval is inconclusive. Start with \(P=1,3,5\). For mechanism, hold \(P=3\) fixed and switch **the same quantized weights** on at visit 1 only, 2 only, 3 only, then all three. Compare the all-visit result with the singleton predictions. That small experiment is worth more than a fourth quantization format.

Check that the hooks actually fire and that the resulting tensor bytes differ. # C. Novelty

11. **Est. — Defensible one-sentence contribution:** *On a native-FP4 looped MoE with controllable depth, we isolate the output cost of requantizing shared experts on individual and repeated visits, and test whether those measurements predict a better held-out allocation at fixed stored bytes.* Use this sentence only if visit-selective intervention works. Without it, replace “isolate” with “measure how blockwise sensitivity varies with deployment depth.” The novelty is the controlled comparison on this numerical and architectural regime. A quant ladder, pooled calibration, router agreement, a greedy solver, nested levels and target-depth selection each have precedents.

12. **Est. — Thesis threshold:** a verified reference, paired block-by-depth estimates on two text distributions, a fixed-depth control and an honest negative result are sufficient. **Workshop threshold:** add a preregistered prediction that can lose, test it on unseen configurations, and transfer one decision from varloop to Mini without refitting. A systematic failure of visit summing that predicts *which allocation reverses rank* would be more informative than a marginal win over one opaque GGUF. Varloop and Mini share training history, so call that within-family transfer. [Training relationship, 20 Nov 2026](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/tml-d24-varloop-research/)
