## B. Prior work on quantization error in looped or weight-shared transformers

The arXiv API returned these for looped-transformer quantization ([query](https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?search_query=all:quantization+AND+all:%22looped+transformer%22&sortBy=submittedDate&sortOrder=descending&max_results=25)). No arXiv paper mentions "Inkling 2" yet.

1. **[LoopQ, arXiv 2605.16343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16343), 8 May 2026.** Loop-aware PTQ at W4A4. Names three sources of degradation, among them "recursive error accumulation". LoopQuant calls it the first work on low-bit quantization of recursive transformers.
2. **[Feedback Exposure, arXiv 2609.30820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30820), 25 Sep 2026** ([full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30820v1)). Single author, v1 only. Per-channel INT4 on Huginn-3.5B hurts the non-residual loop-entry adapter most. One-step GPTQ Hessians miss later-step directions; summing Hessians over recurrence steps beats one-step GPTQ and RTN on nine checkpoints. It does report per-step quantities: a per-step reconstruction proxy that stays nearly constant over 32 steps, and per-step Hessian ranks. It perturbs by component (adapter or core), always on all steps. It has no condition where the weights differ on one step only, no MoE and no GGUF.
3. **[Tilted Bowl, arXiv 2609.39277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39277), 30 Sep 2026** ([full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39277v1)). Tests the accumulation account on 33 models in five families. Finding: a fixed rounding error does not accumulate when the loop settles. It contrasts a fixed error (noise added to the weights once) with a fresh error (noise drawn anew at every call), and it runs the last loops at higher weight precision. So pass-specific weight precision already exists in the literature. Its own limitation statement: the fresh error sits in the activations, the fixed error in the weights.
4. **[Loopy, arXiv 2610.05265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05265), 4 Oct 2026.** Candidate rankings change with recurrent depth, so it selects the shared low-bit representation by final loss at the deployment depth.
5. **[LoopQuant, arXiv 2610.16368](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16368v1), 12 Oct 2026.** Code public. Its ILPA step allocates **activation** precision per loop from Shapley-style marginals and a pairwise term that flags "stronger-than-additive degradation" when two loops are low precision together. That is a per-loop additivity analysis, on activations and on Huginn, Ouro and Nanbeige. Appendix A.3.9 plots layer-wise representation error with pass boundaries marked.
6. **[Dory1.0-4B, arXiv 2611.03998](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.03998v1), 2 Nov 2026.** A looped nGPT trained in NVFP4 with its own PTQ and QAD section. Not read beyond the abstract and contents.
7. Also listed, KV-cache only and not read: LoopVQ (2611.05304), LoopKV (2611.04137), ResidualQuant (2610.10381).

Addendum A's linearity theorem (arXiv 2411.17525) was not rechecked here.

An earlier attempt, #20060, is closed. It concerns the activation type, not NVFP4 weights. Active NVFP4 work is on CUDA ([#32610](https://api.github.com/repos/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/32610)).
- **Unsloth's fork** (release `b12588-mix-c0c3de8`, 20 Nov) carries the same Metal exclusion ([source file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unslothai/llama.cpp/b12588-mix-c0c3de8/ggml/src/ggml-metal/ggml-metal-device.m), [releases](https://api.github.com/repos/unslothai/llama.cpp/releases?per_page=5)).

Files built from standard GGUF types (bartowski, ggml-org IQ4_NL, AtomicChat) probably run fully on Metal too; I did not dump their tensor types.

## Things to fix before any release

- `README.md` line 2 says "the first looped transformer aware quantization attempt". That is false (LoopQ, May 2026) and the CHANGELOG card rules already forbid "first".
- The Metal advantage could vanish with one merge of #20456 or a successor. State the llama.cpp commit next to any "runs fully on Metal" line.

## Verdict

The release slot for a small Mini GGUF is crowded and the allocation idea is no longer special: Unsloth, bartowski, AtomicChat, APEX and several MLX repos all ship mixed-precision files with KLD tables, and LookAtMyQuant claims a visit-level sensitivity measurement on Mini itself at a smaller size. In the literature, loop-aware calibration (LoopQ, Feedback Exposure), depth-aware selection (Loopy), per-loop activation-precision allocation with an interaction term (LoopQuant), fixed-versus-fresh noise and pass-specific weight precision (Tilted Bowl) are all published. What I found nowhere: a causal per-visit decomposition of **weight** quantization error, with the same quantized expert block applied on pass 1 only, pass 2 only, pass 3 only and all passes, an additivity residual, a layer-split control without weight sharing, and tied versus independent weight noise per pass. Nobody has done that on a production looped MoE, on Inkling 2 Mini, or inside llama.cpp, and AtomicChat's card states the accumulation hypothesis as untested. The per-slot llama.cpp patch that makes this test possible also appears to be new. "Runs fully on Metal" still holds against Unsloth's NVFP4-carrying files as of today's master, but it is a narrow, perishable edge and not unique among GGUFs. The scientific result is the novel part; the release candidate is one more mixed-precision file unless it wins the pre-registered gate.
