## B. What fits the next 1–3 days

11. **I/V — Establish a fair control first.** Release b12600 contains the Inkling architecture. The public **#32704 page showed open**. Its author explains that loop slots were copied before expert scales were attached, corrupting passes two and three. Record the actual patched commit for both contestants. V, project: Q8_0 repeat KL was zero; whole-model IQ4_NL was 0.0162. The 0–5 and 6–11 low-bit block KLs were 0.0279 and 0.0624, but both gate/up and down changed. Those runs cannot identify the contribution of either projection. First splice **only** an alternative down tensor into UD-IQ3_XXS, leaving its gate/up bytes intact. [b12600](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12600) · [scale-fix PR](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32704) · [Unsloth files](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF/tree/main)

12. **I/Est. — Fix Q2_0 scale selection.** The public decoder uses \(d\{-1,0,1,2\}\) with one FP16 scale per 64 weights. The reference encoder sets \(d=\max|w|\), so ordinary finite inputs never select \(2d\). The weighted entry point calls the same encoder. For each block, take the matching expert’s 64 input-channel imatrix values \(h_i\). Try several clipped scales and both scale signs. Assign nearest legal levels \(c_i\), refit \(d=\sum h_iw_ic_i/\sum h_ic_i^2\), then reassign. Handle zero denominators, round the scale to FP16, and score the **decoded** candidates, including stock Q2_0. Pack the winning codes directly. Compare stock, optimized-unweighted and optimized-weighted versions to isolate the imatrix benefit. **Est.** Allow 2–4 engineering hours and 15–60 minutes of M5 execution for a compiled one-layer pilot. Risk: even optimized four-level blocks may lose too much. One down-layer conversion from 4.5 to 2.25 bpw frees **95,551,488 payload bytes**. Test what those bytes buy elsewhere. [llama.cpp encoder and decoder](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/ggml/src/ggml-quants.c) · [block definitions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h)

13. **I/Est. — Test native-grid recoding alongside Q2_0.** Decode the *released* expert weights, accounting once for every external expert scale. NVFP4 has E2M1 values with an E4M3-family scale per 16; MXFP4 has E2M1 with a power-of-two scale per 32. For each adjacent pair of source groups, enumerate legal destination exponents, choose nearest codes and measure exact matches and weighted error. IQ4_NL uses a different 16-entry table with **no zero entry** and one FP16 scale per 32. Fit its scale and codes separately. A four-value E2M1 subset reusing the original scales is exact only where every occupied value belongs to that subset. A new subset decoder requires runtime support. MXFP4 saves **10,616,832 bytes per converted down layer**; IQ4_NL uses the same 4.5 bpw as native down. **Est.** Allow 1–3 M5 hours for sampled scans, packing and one-layer validation. IQ4_NL is the strongest immediate speed-control candidate; MXFP4 offers a smaller quality-for-bytes trade. Verify routed Metal dispatch for each type. [GGML grids and scales](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h) · [MR-GPTQ scale analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23202)

14. **I — Full GPTQ needs routed input correlations.** Extend the imatrix callback at `MUL_MAT_ID`. Record physical layer, visit, expert IDs and matching operand rows. Use the actual gate/up input and, separately, the post-gating activation entering down. Accumulate uncentered FP32 \(H_e=\sum_p X_{e,p}^{T}X_{e,p}\) by batched matrix multiplication. Gate and up can share a Gram matrix when their input is identical. Preserve counts and sum before normalizing. For an isolated down expert’s contribution to the routed output, weighting each row by its router coefficient before forming the Gram gives squared-coefficient weighting; check whether the captured operand already includes that factor. The imatrix diagonal discards the off-diagonal terms that permit OBS error compensation. [llama.cpp collector](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/tools/imatrix/imatrix.cpp) · [GPTQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323) · [DASLab MoE collector precedent](https://github.com/IST-DASLab/MoE-Quant)

15. **I/Est. — Size and solve the pilot before scaling it.** For 128 experts, \(128\times2304^2\times4\) is **2,717,908,992 bytes, or 2.53125 GiB**, per gate/up layer. Down at width 1,152 adds 0.633 GiB. Both across 24 layers occupy **75.94 GiB** on disk, before factors, activations or workspaces. Stream one layer and factor one expert at a time. Damp by a fraction of the mean diagonal, handle inactive channels, Cholesky-factor the damped inverse, round a column on the legal grid, and propagate its residual to remaining columns. Fall back to the diagonal fit for under-sampled experts. Li’s visit sum is exactly the Gram for **summed local reconstruction on a fixed trajectory**. It omits downstream message amplification, rerouting and cross-visit loss curvature. **Est.** Budget 0.5–1 day for capture and parity checks, then 1–4 G4 hours for a few selected layers after timing one expert. Same-byte quality gains are plausible; a whole-model win in 48 hours is speculative. [trajectory-calibration derivation](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30820v1) · [GPTQ implementation](https://github.com/IST-DASLab/gptq)

16. **I/Est. — Preserve optimized codes through export.** Q4_0 permits one signed integer choice per coordinate at fixed scale; IQ4_NL permits one table index; Q2_0 permits four scalar choices. K-quants permit scalar codes subject to their shared subscales and, where applicable, minima. IQ2_XXS/XS/S and IQ3_XXS require legal vector entries and format-specific signs/scales. Independent scalar GPTQ decisions generally cannot be packed into those layouts. Search legal codewords jointly or keep the existing llama.cpp encoding. For GSQ-style refinement, replay captured inputs on one expert, initialize from its packed block, optimize nearby assignment logits and scales against the released expert’s outputs, hard-project, pack, and compare against the simpler fit on held-out activations. Passing the fitted floats through a generic quantizer can erase the improvement. **Est.** Allow 1–4 G4 hours for selected experts plus packer work. The main risk is a local reconstruction gain too small to affect model KL. [GSQ method](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18556v2) · [GGML format layouts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h)

17. **I/Est. — Make the allocator pass-aware while charging physical bytes once.** HIGGS predicts expected perplexity change from squared relative weight errors under small perturbations, smoothness and an approximately block-isotropic scaled Hessian, with local optimality or unbiasedness addressing the first-order term. For a tied perturbation, cross-visit terms can survive. Measure each singleton pass, all passes tied, and independent Gaussian perturbations at matched per-visit norms across several seeds and amplitudes. Record \(K_{\rm tied}-\sum_p K_p\). Use measured tied costs for shared tensors in the release DP; test its predicted cost on complete allocations and try budget-neutral swaps. Separate per-pass weights cost actual extra storage. **Est.** DP should take seconds to minutes with a sparse byte frontier. Evaluation dominates: one 65,536-token sweep takes \(65{,}536/r\) seconds at measured scoring throughput \(r\), plus I/O. Screen on fewer calibration chunks first. [linearity assumptions and theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.17525v1) · [RCO objective and discrete solver](https://github.com/IST-DASLab/RCO)
